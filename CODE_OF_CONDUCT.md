# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We — maintainers and contributors — pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps the community:

- being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments,
personal or political attacks, publishing others' private information, and any
conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **studio.supernovae@gmail.com**. Maintainers
will review and respond, and may remove, edit, or reject contributions — or ban
contributors — for behaviour they deem inappropriate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
