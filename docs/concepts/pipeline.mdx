The ladder replaces brute-force retry loops with a **fixed, declared
attempt order** — no RNG anywhere. Budget and cancellation are honored
between attempts; engines are panic-isolated.

```text
ImageInput (Encoded | Rgba8 | Luma8)
  → validate limits
  → alpha flatten — transparent pixels composite over a declared
    background BEFORE anything else (auto = the design's intended
    placement; opaque inputs untouched, bit for bit)
  → BT.601 luma · cap to max_engine_side (2048)
  S1 pyramid    ≤512px downscale — cheapest, often best on artistic
  S2 direct     full-resolution luma
  S3 enhance    otsu · invert · contrast stretch · R/G/B channels
  S4 deep       15 rungs: 12 contrast boosts + 3 morphological closes,
                then a size × stretch × binarization grid
  S5 rescue     errors-and-erasures RS over grids the engines read
                but could not decode
  → (alpha auto, zero detections) retry over the OPPOSITE background
  → reconcile reads by content identity → score → alpha placement
    envelope → ScanReport
```

A stage that finds something still **completes** (consensus inside the
stage is preserved), then the ladder stops — unless every QR code read so
far only signals an ECI it could not locate: then the later stages run, so
that one of them can locate the declarations. Reads the acceptance holds
(below) do not end the walk either. Each later stage exists for a
documented failure class:

| Stage | Recovers |
|---|---|
| S1 | over-sampled artistic renders (downscale averages texture into modules) |
| S3 invert | light-on-dark symbols (polarity travels with the geometry — `meta.inverted`) |
| S3 channels | color art where one RGB plane carries the structure |
| S4 boosts | low-contrast art texture (multiplicative contrast crush) |
| S4 morphological closes | **blob/dot pixel styles** — gaps between sub-module blobs read as noise until closed |
| S5 rescue | **logo-occluded symbols** past the engines' RS error wall |
| alpha flatten + retry | **transparent exports** — the class where the verdict used to depend on which tool wrote the PNG (canvas stores black under the alpha, editors often white); the flatten pins it, the opposite-background retry kills the false "no detection", and `report.alpha.envelope` answers *over which backgrounds the design keeps decoding* with tested probes |

## From reads to detections

Engines report **reads**, never text. The scanner error-corrects and
parses QR streams itself, so `content.raw` is the original payload and
the declarations (ECI, FNC1, Kanji and Hanzi segments) are read once:

- **Sources.** The grid `rqrr` decoded (in the orientation that
  error-corrects); grids `rqrr` detected but could not decode — FNC1,
  Hanzi, structured-append and damaged symbols — re-derived by the
  scanner; the codewords of `rxing`'s ZXing-lineage reader; S5 rescue
  streams; and the re-read.
- **The re-read.** When a read's declarations are not observed yet — it
  signals an ECI or FNC1, or its bytes are not UTF-8 — the scanner
  samples the symbol again at the corners the engine reported and parses
  it to locate them: at most 16 times per scan, with no time budget and
  nothing added to the trace. A port read at EC level L or M is re-read
  on versions 1 and 2 for its correction margin.
- **Refusals.** A stream the scanner can error-correct but that parses as
  malformed is refused whichever decoder reads it, and a tolerant reading
  of the same symbol is withheld.
- **Reconciliation.** At the end of the walk, reads are grouped into the
  distinct contents the scanner can tell apart — same symbology, bytes,
  FNC1 position and ECI declarations, where they lie — never by `text`.
  Engines are the union of a detection's reads.

**Acceptance.** Every read is weighed over the whole walk before it may
settle a stage, stop S5 or be published:

- **Correction margin.** A QR content whose every measured margin is
  negative — corrected past the ISO/IEC 18004 Annex B reserve, which only
  happens on versions 1 and 2 at low EC levels — is refused; one read
  with a margin of zero or more admits it.
- **Independent confirmation.** UPC-E, DataBar, DataBar Expanded and
  PDF417 at EC level 0, 1 or none — the symbologies a reader can invent
  from noise — wait for a second read of the same symbology and bytes
  from a plane at least 1.25× larger or smaller; right after the first
  read the scanner decodes that symbology once on the plane at three
  quarters of its size (not yet for DataBar, until the core crate
  requires rxing 0.9.3). A read still waiting when the walk ends is
  refused.
- **Read checks.** A DataBar text is exactly fourteen digits; a plain port
  read whose bytes are not its text is refused.

| Engine | Reads | Contributes |
|---|---|---|
| `rxing` (zxing-cpp port; ZXing-lineage reader only when the port finds nothing) | every symbology, light-on-dark included | the EC level; corner points used internally |
| `rqrr` (quirc lineage) | QR, mirrored grids retried | geometry, version, mask and the raw stream that feeds UEC and S5 |
| `rescue` (ours) | QR grids no engine decoded | the S5 stage only (`engines: ["rescue"]`) |

## S5 — the erasure rescue

The flagship. Both engines treat every corrupted codeword as a
full-price *error* and die at `t > ⌊(d−p)/2⌋`. But the geometry engine
often still READS the grid and its format info where RS fails — that
sampled bitstream is recoverable:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confidence per codeword">
    Each codeword's 8 modules are sampled at their centers through the
    detection homography; the worst |sample − threshold| margin is the
    codeword's confidence (logo zones and art texture sit near the
    threshold → low margin).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Erasure marking">
    Low-confidence codewords become **erasures** — known-position losses
    that cost HALF an unknown error in the capacity law `e + 2t ≤ d − p`.
    A block corrected with erasures must keep two parity codewords unspent
    beyond the Annex B reserve, so a wrong correction passes about once in
    65 536.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Errors-and-erasures Reed-Solomon">
    Forney 1965: erasure locator, modified syndromes, Berlekamp-Massey,
    Chien search, Forney magnitudes — then a hard syndrome re-check and a
    structural bitstream parse. Refusal-biased: no guess ever reaches the
    report.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Rescued detections carry `engines: ["rescue"]` and a `"rescue"` entry in
`trace.stages`. **Measured: center occlusion tolerance grows from 20% to
30% radius (2.2× the area) on v5-H.**

<Note>
Determinism contract: same bytes + same config + same scanner build ⇒ the
same attempt sequence, bit for bit on a given platform. A wall-clock budget
makes the *cut point* machine-dependent, and the report says when it cut:
a planned stage, attempt or rescue candidate the deadline prevents sets
`trace.budget_exhausted: true` (partial); `false` is the unbounded report.
The grid re-derivations, the re-read, the end-of-walk pass and the
confirmation decodes have no budget and never set it. Cross-platform, libm transcendentals may differ in
the last ulp; rxing's PDF417 decoder breaks ties in randomized order (the
one engine exception).
</Note>
